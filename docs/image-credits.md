## Photos that would be better from Chris

Real local photos beat stock for trust and for local SEO. Highest value first:
1. **Hero**: a real south metro home with a recent roof, ideally in West Linn or Lake Oswego, on an overcast day (landscape, 2400px+).
2. **Moss**: a mossy roof in the area (very common; any neighbor's roof with permission).
3. **City pages**: one recognizable shot per city (Oswego Lake, the West Linn waterfront or Willamette neighborhood, the Oregon City elevator or McLoughlin district, Milwaukie Bay Park, the Clackamas River in Gladstone). The current West Linn shot is a historic house, not a skyline.
4. **Flat roof**: the current photo is a commercial rooftop; a residential low-slope roof (garage, modern addition) fits better.
5. **Once rented**: the renter's own job photos (with permission), which can then be labeled as their work.

Swap a photo by replacing `src/assets/photos/<slot>.jpg` (same name) and updating this table and `src/data/imageCredits.js`.
