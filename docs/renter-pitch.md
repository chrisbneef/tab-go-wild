## 3. Call script (draft)

**Opener (15 sec)**
"Hi, is this [Name]? This is Chris, I'm local in West Linn. I'll be quick. I run a roofing website for
Lake Oswego, West Linn and Oregon City that's getting homeowners calling for roof quotes, and I'm looking
for one roofer to send those calls to. Do you have two minutes?"

**If yes**
"Last month it got [X] calls and [Y] form requests: leaks, replacements, moss, gutters. Right now they go
to my voicemail because I'm not a roofer. I want them going to someone with good reviews, and you have
[57 five-star reviews].

Here's the deal: I forward every call straight to your phone for a week, free. Nothing to sign. If you like
them, your company name and CCB number go on the site, and it's one flat monthly fee, month to month. No
per-lead billing."

**Questions to ask them**
- "Which cities do you want more work in?"
- "What jobs do you want more of: replacements, repairs, gutters?"
- "How do you get leads now? Angi, referrals, your own site?"
- "If I sent you [10] good calls a month, roughly what's that worth to you?"

**Objections**
- *"I get enough work."* → "Totally fair. Most roofers say winter repairs are steady but replacements
  dip. This is exclusive, so it's work you'd otherwise lose to whoever ranks. Want to see the call log?"
- *"Leads are junk."* → "That's why it's a free week and every call is recorded. You judge."
- *"How much?"* → "Depends on volume. Right now it's [$X]/month flat. Cheaper than [N] shared Angi leads
  and nobody else gets the call."
- *"Is this Angi?"* → "No. It's one website, one roofer, your name on it. No bidding against others."
- *"Do I have to sign a contract?"* → "No. Month to month. Stop paying and the calls stop."

**Close**
"Can I start forwarding calls to you on [Monday]? What number should they ring?"

## 4. Simple terms to put in the rental agreement (have a lawyer review)

- Flat monthly fee, billed in advance, month to month; 30-day notice to cancel either way
- Exclusivity: one roofer per service area while the agreement is active
- Renter provides and keeps current: legal business name, active Oregon CCB #, insurance; owner may
  suspend if the license lapses (the site can't advertise for an unlicensed contractor)
- Owner keeps the domain, site and tracking number; calls are forwarded to the renter while active
- Renter supplies only real reviews/photos and owns their own Google Business Profile
- Optional purchase option after [12] months at [price]
- No per-lead pricing; no guarantee of call volume (share the tracked history instead)
